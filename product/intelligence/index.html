Partner intelligence

Who needs my attention today, why, and what should I do?

Refficks answers that question every morning, in priority order, with the evidence sitting beside every recommendation. It is the screen the rest of the product exists to fill.

The Actions screen

Four kinds of recommendation, ranked by cost

Not four dashboards. One list, sorted, with structured evidence attached to every row.

Activate this partner

Somebody you approved who has not started. The evidence is the elapsed time, the zero counts, and how long your programme usually takes.

Contact that one

Somebody who was producing and has gone quiet. The evidence is their old rhythm against their current silence.

Consider a raise for this one

Somebody outperforming their plan. The evidence is their revenue, their conversion rate against the programme median, and the plan they are still on.

Review that one

Somebody whose numbers do not look like everyone else’s. The evidence is the pattern, stated plainly, with nothing withheld and nothing accused.

Precisely: evidence is structured, not prose — each item is a named factor with a value, so the same recommendation is reproducible tomorrow and auditable next quarter.

Dismissal

“Not now” means not tomorrow morning either

The fastest way to make a recommendation engine useless is to re-raise everything every morning until the operator stops reading it.

A dismissed recommendation stays dismissed. If the same situation genuinely returns months later — the partner went quiet again, the rate is out of line again — it is raised again, because by then it is news.

Precisely: dismissal is recorded against the situation, not the notification. Nothing is suppressed silently, and nothing is repeated to fill space.

Scoring

Three numbers, and the arithmetic behind each

Every partner is scored nightly on three axes. Score is their rank inside your programme, expressed as a percentile rather than a grade — an A in a weak programme means nothing.

Potential is room to grow, and it is deliberately not a function of current revenue, so your largest partner does not automatically look like your best opportunity.

Risk is what needs a second look.

Precisely: every factor shows its weight, its value, the points it contributed, and one sentence of evidence. Weights are configuration, not code.

Lifecycle

Stages are earned, not assigned

Nobody drags a partner into a stage. A click onboards them. A sale activates them. Silence makes them dormant. The stage is a description of what they have actually done.

With two exceptions. Refficks never sets applicant and never sets rejected, because letting someone into your programme is a decision a person makes.

Precisely: stage transitions are derived from events in the ledger, so a partner’s stage and their history can never disagree.

The funnel

It names who is stuck, not just how many

Signed up but never clicked. Clicked but never sold. The funnel reports the count, the median days between each stage, and the partners sitting in each gap.

A percentage tells you that you have a problem. A list of names tells you what to do before lunch.

Precisely: medians, not means, so a single outlier cannot flatter or wreck the number.

Optional assistance

It can argue against a recommendation. It cannot move a penny.

Refficks has an assistant. It is off by default, you can switch it off for your organization even where the deployment has it on, and the interesting part is not what it does — it is what it has been structurally prevented from doing.

It summarises a partner, explains a recommendation — including the case against it — and drafts outreach that you edit before anything is sent.

Everything it writes is labelled “Written by AI”, with the model named. Every interaction is recorded, including the failures.

Precisely: partner-typed text reaches the model as data inside a delimited record, and responses are validated against a fixed shape before any screen shows them. Prompt injection cannot change what renders.

Cannot

Touch money

Structurally, not by policy. The interface it is reached through has no tools, no database access and no write path. It cannot approve, reverse or suspend anything no matter what it is told to do.

Cannot

See a bank account

Partner payout details never reach the model. Provably: there is a test asserting their absence from the context it is given.

Cannot

Be switched on behind your back

Off by default. A merchant can switch it off for their organization even when the deployment it runs on has it enabled.

The guarantees

No machine learning. Every recommendation shows reasoning you can disagree with.

A recommendation you cannot argue with is a recommendation you cannot trust. Refficks runs on deterministic rules, so every reason is legible and every weight is yours to change.

Refuses to

Say “fraud”

A risk finding says “Review recommended” and shows the pattern. It does not accuse anyone, and there is no apply button on it. What happens next is your call, on your evidence.

Refuses to

Change money on one click

Raising a rate or moving a tier needs an explicit confirmation that names the numbers, and shows what the change costs a month at the current run rate.

Refuses to

Nag

“Not now” sticks. The same recommendation is not put back in front of you tomorrow morning because the engine had nothing better to say.
