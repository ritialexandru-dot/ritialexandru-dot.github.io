How it works

A morning with Refficks

Three screens, in the order you meet them.

1

It ranks what needs you

Not a chart. A queue, ordered by what each situation is costing you, with the reason written on the row.

Precisely: deterministic rules, not machine learning. Weights are configuration, not code — so you can read every reason and disagree with it.

2

It shows its evidence

Every score opens into its factors: the weight, the value, the points, and one sentence of evidence for each.

Precisely: every attribution decision is stored with its full reasoning — every touch considered, which were eligible, which won, which lost and why, and a confidence score.

3

You decide. It never acts alone

Money recommendations have no one-click apply. Raising a rate takes a confirmation that names the numbers and shows what the change costs at your current run rate.

Precisely: an automation may send an email, add a tag, or enrol a partner in a campaign. It cannot touch money. Ever.

Trust

Software that refuses things

A new product has no logos to show you. What it has instead is a list of the things it will not do, and the reasons are the product.

Refuses to

Edit history

A refund writes a reversal beside the original entry. Nothing is ever rewritten: not a commission, not an attribution, not a raw event.

Precisely: events, attributions, commission entries and audit logs are append-only. Corrections supersede; they do not overwrite.

Refuses to

Let the AI touch money

The assistant cannot approve, reverse or suspend anything, no matter what it is told to do.

Precisely: structurally, not by policy. The interface it is reached through has no tools, no database access and no write path.

Refuses to

Move your money

Refficks builds the batch, tells you who cannot be paid and why, and exports a CSV your bank can use. It never moves money itself.

Precisely: there is always a step where a person can say no.

Refuses to

Print confident zeroes

An empty programme says “no data yet” rather than “$0.00”. A first month says “no earlier period to compare” rather than “+100%”.

Precisely: a number that has not been earned is not displayed as though it had been.

For the developer they forward this to

Retries are free. Duplicates are impossible.

Send the same event twice — a webhook retry, a double-submitted form, a replayed queue job — and it resolves to the original. Idempotency is enforced by the database, not by application code that hopes for the best.

Precisely: money is integer minor units plus an ISO currency, everywhere. Never floats, never a silent conversion between currencies.
